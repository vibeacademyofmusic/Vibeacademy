# Historical access — owner decisions V1

Status: OWNER_APPROVED
Scope: Security rollout, reports and Student/Parent/Teacher portals.
Source: explicit owner answers in the Master Execution Plan V2 task.

## Learners and parents

After a pause or class completion, students may view their own attendance history, approved reports and debt; parents may view those records for explicitly linked children. Accounts and the relevant parent-child relationship must remain ACTIVE. Current ACTIVE enrollment is not required solely to retain this historical access. Valid roles, permissions and record-level ownership checks still apply. This does not expose draft reports, staff notes, other learners or unrelated financial records.

## Former teachers

After teaching assignment ends, teachers retain access only to sessions they actually taught and journals they authored. Historical teaching does not grant access to a learner's current profile or current class roster. ACTIVE account and valid authorization remain required. An independently valid current teaching assignment can separately grant its normal current scope.

These decisions supersede earlier assumptions about historical relationship access. They do not authorize production migrations or changes.
